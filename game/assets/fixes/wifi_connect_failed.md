# Wi-Fi Connection Failed

The computer tried to switch networks, but the connection failed or dropped.

## Check

- **Password.** If the network's password changed, forget the network in your system Wi-Fi settings, then connect to it by hand once with the new one.
- **Signal.** Weak signal makes connecting time out. Move closer to the device.
- **The device itself.** If it rebooted or lost power, wait for it to come back up, then reconnect.
- **Home network.** Leaving the device relies on the **Home Network** name. Check that it's spelled the way your system shows it.
